# V0.9 Audio System Notes

V0.9 does not add third-party audio sources beyond the audio assets already shipped/documented in V0.8. It changes playback architecture and mix behavior.

- All music and SFX now route through one foreground-only Web Audio manager.
- User-visible Music/SFX volume sliders scale internal tuned gains.
- V0.9 lowers the music tuned maximum by approximately 35% and raises the synthesized selector tick by approximately 25%.
- Background/hidden pages stop active audio and suspend the context.
- Missed scheduled SFX are discarded via authoritative timestamp freshness checks.
- The existing pitch-preserved Final Two music render is retained.

See `licenses/V0.8_AUDIO_NOTES.md` and the retained source/license artifacts for the underlying audio-source notes.
