TILES v0.8 — FULL PIXEL COMPETITION POLISH

Gameplay / room flow
- Final-destination occupancy is now the only player blocking rule. Moving characters may continue to cross/slide past each other without recoil or collision state.
- A human whose final target remains occupied stops one tile short and receives a private double red target flash plus a subtle blocked-destination sound.
- Bots use the exact same authoritative final-destination rule, without human-only UI feedback.
- Post-match flow is now podium -> ordinary lobby. The separate REMATCH state/button has been removed; players press READY again.
- Public Quick Play rooms with zero connected humans are no longer matchmaker-eligible. An explicit last-human leave abandons the room immediately; unexpected disconnects retain reconnect grace.
- Bot username pool expanded to exactly 250 names: 85 human-style names/variants + 165 arcade/gamer names.

Pixel-art presentation
- Added local Tiles Pixel bitmap-style font and applied it across dynamic gamer tags, room codes, buttons, tutorial copy, HUD, settings, podium, selector text and inputs.
- Converted buttons, inputs, panels, toasts and controls to stepped pixel geometry; selector wheel/pointer now use hard pixel silhouettes.
- Fixed player-card trophy/count spacing.
- Fixed podium winner animation so it snaps to one sprite frame at a time instead of sliding through the sheet.
- Pixelated the Apogee Lab mark while retaining its recognizable shape.
- Official app icon package updated from the approved Tiles character + logo icon.

Audio / feedback
- Added per-tile mechanical shuffle click synchronized with the left-to-right tile flip wave.
- Added ceramic break cue for doomed-tile destruction.
- Added blocked-destination error cue.
- Added one-shot FINAL TWO swoosh synchronized to the framed fly-through banner.
- Added podium fanfare, triggered after the podium has visually appeared.
- Added pitch-preserved Final Two soundtrack variant at roughly +15 BPM and a short crossfade at the equivalent musical position.

Final Two
- FINAL TWO announcement/swoosh occurs only once per match, on the first transition to exactly two survivors.
- Persistent Final Two atmosphere remains active afterward: brighter/faster shooting particles, hollow-center pixel stars, stronger arena glow and faster music.
